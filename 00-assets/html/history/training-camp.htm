How scores and historical comparisons work

Player impact = POT delta × pre-camp POT ÷ 100, where POT delta is after-camp POT minus pre-camp POT. Team camp impact sums the player contributions. Switch the weight to OVR to multiply that same POT delta by pre-camp OVR instead. Ratings above 100 retain their full weight. Net skill change is supporting context and does not affect impact.

Declines count negatively. Ties use average POT change, then the share of players whose POT increased. Team history ranks the average camp impact, giving each recorded camp equal weight. Raw POT delta is summed per camp; franchise rows show both its average per camp and cumulative total.

Includes matched roster players with OVR of at least 50 in either snapshot, including players with no change. Mascots and free agents are excluded. Players without a matching identity or complete ratings are excluded and counted in the coverage column. Players are credited to their team in the later snapshot; team history groups by roster identity.

Historical comparisons use the same adjacent archived season snapshots as Player Camp Changes. They can include other rating changes between snapshots and players who changed teams. The current camp compares live ratings against the saved PreTC snapshot, so it can change as the live database updates.
